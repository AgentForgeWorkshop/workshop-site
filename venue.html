Getting There from Changi Airport

Taxi or ride-hailing (recommended, 35–45 min)

Take a taxi at the airport, or book a ride with an app. We recommend downloading and registering before you travel, while you have internet:

  • Grab
  • Zig
  • Tada

Train (about 2 hours)

If you are travelling light, board at Changi Airport (CG2), transfer at Tanah Merah (EW4), and ride to Boon Lay or Pioneer station. From there, take a taxi or bus to NEC.
